# Source policy

## Priority

Use the most specific and authoritative official source available.

1. Official errata / FAQ / ruling for a specific card or exact interaction.
2. Current official rules and glossary.
3. Official card database for printed characteristics and card text.
4. Official tournament pages for formats, restrictions and tournament governance.
5. Official social channels for announcements and time-sensitive context.

## Rules

- Community material must not override an official source.
- A card-specific official FAQ/ruling is preferred over a generic inference from the rules.
- Time-sensitive facts must carry `temporal = "Да"` and a `snapshot_date`.
- If official sources do not support a requested fact, the correct behavior is abstention, not guessing.
- When a source changes materially, update the dataset version and preserve the previous revision in Git history.
